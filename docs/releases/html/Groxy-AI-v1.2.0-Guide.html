Groxy AI v1.2.0

Brand DNA & Voice Setup Wizard + Batch Content and Product Campaigns

Implementation status: Verified; dual-repository publication pending • SemVer: MINOR, backwards-compatible feature release • Runtime: GRAB 1.1.0, Grex JS 1.0.0, GrabLab CMS 1.0.1, Grex Theme Builder 1.0.0

Groxy AI now learns a business's audience, voice, local focus, offers, pricing rules, guarantees, and design tokens once. Teams can use that profile to prepare up to 40 related blog or product drafts from a short brief. Every item is audited and reviewed before it can be sent to the CMS.

1. Set up Brand DNA once

Open the Groxy dashboard and answer the ten short, step-by-step questions. The wizard captures business identity, audience needs, service area and local search terms, voice weights, forbidden phrases, preferred calls to action, offer types, exact prices, guarantee details, and the selected Design Style Pack and --grex-* tokens.

Saving requires an explicit submit. Groxy stores the structured profile at storage/brand-dna.v1.json and a readable copy at storage/BRAND-DNA.md. Revision checks prevent an older browser session from silently replacing newer guidance. Prices remain decimal strings; JavaScript floating-point values are rejected.

2. Prepare a campaign preview

Choose blog posts or products, enter a one-sentence campaign brief, and select between deterministic built-in generation, local Ollama, or a configured allowlisted BYOK provider. Deterministic generation has no API fee. Ollama uses loopback and falls back cleanly when it is unavailable. BYOK targets must satisfy the configured outbound allowlist.

Each preview item includes a unique title and slug, semantic content, and escaped Article or Product JSON-LD. Every candidate must pass the 22-rule, four-pillar auditor with a score of at least 90 (Grade A or A+). Product prices keep their exact decimal-string representation.

3. Review and approve drafts

Groxy stages an immutable preview and issues a one-time btok_* approval token bound to the batch ID, item count, and SHA-256 batch digest. Applying the preview requires the CSRF token and matching approval token. Invalid or mismatched tokens return 403; a replay returns 409.

On approval, Groxy sends each item to GrabLab CMS through create_entry_draft with status_enforced: "draft". The feature never publishes live content automatically.
