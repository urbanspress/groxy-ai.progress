Groxy AI v0.2.0 Walkthrough Guide

Pre-Publish SEO & Performance Grade Review Gate & “Let Groxy Boost This” Side-by-Side Diff Engine

Release Series: Post-1.0 Step 4 • Runtime: GRAB v1.1.0 + Grex JS v1.0.0 • SemVer Tier: Pre-1.0 Feature Release

What Groxy AI v0.2.0 Adds in Plain English: Before a post, product, or page goes live, editors can opt in to Groxy AI’s Pre-Publish Grade Review Gate. Groxy scores the draft from A+ to F across 22 deterministic rules, then gives the author two clear choices: click Make This Live immediately as-is, or click Let Groxy Boost This to preview a clean side-by-side Before vs. After Diff that lifts failing drafts to A/A+ without altering prices or publishing anything until the editor clicks Approve & Make This Live.

1. Opt-In Pre-Publish Grade Review Gate

  • Endpoint: CSRF-protected POST /api/groxy/prepublish-review (src/groxy.grab & public/groxy/booster.js).
  • Opt-In Behavior: When groxy_assist_enabled is false, the gate returns gate_status: "bypassed" with zero interference. When true, Groxy runs the 22-rule 4-pillar auditor and opens an accessible, zero-wrapper-<div> review screen.
  • Explicit Human Choices: Authors choose either make_live (Make This Live) or boost (Let Groxy Boost This).

2. Deterministic “Let Groxy Boost This” Remediation & Side-by-Side Diff

POST /api/groxy/boost deterministically fixes flagged issues across both the CMS entry and the Grex visual layout while guaranteeing safety:

Remediated Area What Groxy AI v0.2.0 Fixes Safety Invariant Enforced
On-Page SEO Expands short titles/excerpts into 45–60 char SEO titles and 120–155 char descriptions; replaces noisy admin slugs with clean kebab-case slugs; strengthens thin copy when needed. Original draft in GrabLab CMS remains 100% untouched during preview.
DOM & Accessibility Converts wrapper <div> nodes to semantic Grex containers (<main>/<section>), fixes skipped H1 → H3 heading levels, adds missing alt attributes, and replaces low-contrast colors with --grex-* / .grex-c-* tokens. Strips scripts, inline on* handlers, and javascript:/data: URLs; re-audits to guarantee divCount === 0 & publish_safe === true.
Exact Numbers & 301 Links Replaces vague click here anchor text and resolves stale /old-guide links pointing at slug_history or redirects to the canonical target. ADR-025c: Preserves base-10 GrabDecimal ("49.99") and GrabInteger ("10042") strings byte-for-byte.

3. Human-Approval Persistence & Ecosystem Compatibility

  • Single-Use Approval Gate: POST /api/groxy/boost/approve requires a valid X-CSRF-Token plus the single-use approval_token issued by /api/groxy/boost. Invalid tokens fail with 403; replayed tokens fail with 409 (GROXY-SAFE-001).
  • Draft-Only & Actor-Attributed Dispatch: Approved boosts save entries via GrabLab CMS MCP create_entry_draft (forced status: "draft") and candidate layouts via Grex Theme Builder POST /api/mcp/layout (actor: "groxy-ai"), keeping the live published site unchanged until final human confirmation.
  • Zero Upstream Modifications: 100% compatible with frozen baselines GRAB v1.1.0, Grex JS v1.0.0, GrabLab CMS v1.0.1, and Grex Theme Builder v1.0.0.
